My timeline of becoming a designer

Age 7

It started with a drawing book my grandma bought me. It sparked my love for art and quickly became my favorite hobby.

High School

My family couldn't afford a laptop, so I sold candy bars at school until I could. That's when I discovered digital design — and fell in love with it.

College

Studied design and found my true calling — using art and technology to help people. That's how I discovered and pursued product design.

Now

Designing products people love, illustrating for fun, and always working on something new.
